Agency FAQ

Can clients review exports without logging in?

Yes. Share read-only links or white-label decks that inherit your agency branding. Clients see polished deliverables without needing a Slate account.

Does Slate keep compliance logs?

Every export is timestamped with requester, client, board context, and attachments. This audit trail lets you prove exactly how AI outputs were generated and approved.

Can I white-label exports for clients?

Yes. Exports can be customized with your agency branding. Deliver professional presentations, implementation guides, or prompt packs that look like they came directly from your team.

How do I manage multiple client workspaces?

Each client gets isolated boards with separate access controls. Your team can switch between client contexts while maintaining clear boundaries. Activity logs track who did what, when.

What AI integrations are available?

Slate exports work with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and any LLM that accepts structured context. Your team can use whatever AI tools they prefer.
