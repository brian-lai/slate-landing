Founder FAQ

How fast can I get started?

Early access includes templates for GTM planning, fundraising, and sprint organization. You can start structuring your first board within minutes of joining.

Can I share boards with investors or contractors?

Yes. Export read-only decks for investor updates, AI-ready prompts for contractors, or invite collaborators to specific boards with activity tracking.

Is Slate suitable for non-technical founders?

Absolutely. Slate is designed for structured thinking, not coding. You organize your ideas in cards, and Slate handles formatting them for AI tools. When you're ready to build, export your specs and hand them to any AI coding assistant.

How does Slate compare to no-code tools?

No-code tools help you build specific outputs. Slate helps you think and plan before building. Use Slate to structure your requirements, then export to AI assistants that can generate code, no-code workflows, or documentation.

Can I export pitch decks directly?

Slate exports structured context that AI tools can transform into pitch decks, one-pagers, or investor updates. The hierarchical format ensures nothing gets lost in translation.
